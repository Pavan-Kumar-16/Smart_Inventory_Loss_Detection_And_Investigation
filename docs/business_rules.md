Rule ID	Business rule
BR-001	Every inventory movement must reference a valid store and SKU.
BR-002	Opening stock must be represented by OPENING movements.
BR-003	Sales and damage reduce inventory.
BR-004	Receipts and legitimate returns increase inventory.
BR-005	Transfers must have matching outgoing and incoming records.
BR-006	Stock adjustments must have an authorized employee and reason.
BR-007	Physical counts must be compared with stock as of the count timestamp.
BR-008	Alerts must contain an explanation and supporting evidence.
BR-009	Investigation status changes must be recorded in history.
BR-010	Ground-truth labels must remain separate from detection inputs.
